## Description

```
A symptom diary for people managing chronic conditions — built so that logging takes seconds, even on a bad day.

WHEN YOU'RE IN PAIN, LOGGING SHOULD BE FAST
Open the app, pick what you're feeling, set the severity, save. Every other field is optional. Log several symptoms in one sitting, each with its own details — because a headache that lasted all day and fatigue that lifted after an hour are different things worth recording differently.

YOUR STORY, READY FOR YOUR APPOINTMENT
Appointments are short and it's hard to summarise months of symptoms from memory. HealthLit turns your entries into a structured summary covering the questions clinicians actually ask: when it started, how it's changed, what you're experiencing, how often and for how long, patterns you've recorded, what seems to help, what else happens alongside, and what you've tried.

Pick any date range, edit any section in your own words, then print it or share it as a PDF.

EVERY NUMBER IS TRACEABLE
Tap any sentence in a report to see the exact entries behind it. Nothing is invented, estimated, or generated by AI — reports are calculated on your device from what you logged, so they work offline and every figure can be checked against your own data.

HealthLit is deliberately cautious about what it claims. It reports what you recorded and how often things occurred together. It does not tell you what caused what.

WORKS WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Everything is stored on your device and every feature works offline. Signing in is optional and only adds a backup so you can move between devices. No ads, no tracking, and your health data is never sold.

ALSO INCLUDED
• Severity trends and frequency charts
• Body map for marking where it hurts
• Custom symptoms beyond the built-in ones
• Medication list, linkable to individual entries
• Full history, searchable and editable
• Light and dark mode

IMPORTANT
HealthLit is a symptom diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose conditions, identify causes, predict outcomes, or recommend treatment. Always speak to a qualified clinician about your health.
```

## Notes for the reviewer

Paste into App Review Information → Notes. Health apps get read carefully; this pre-empts the usual questions.

```
WHAT THIS APP IS
HealthLit is a symptom diary for people managing chronic conditions. Users log symptoms and severity, and the app summarises their own entries into a structured document to bring to a medical appointment.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED — NO DEMO CREDENTIALS NEEDED
Every feature works without signing in. Please launch the app and use it directly; nothing is gated behind an account. Sign-in is optional and only adds cloud backup.

TO SEE THE MAIN FEATURE QUICKLY
The report feature requires logged data, which is normally accumulated over time. To see it immediately:
1. Open the Profile tab
2. Under "Developer Tools", tap "Load Demo Data" (fills the app with sample entries)
3. Go to the Home tab and tap "Your Story"
4. Tap "Generate story"
5. Tap any sentence containing a number to see the entries it was calculated from

NO DIAGNOSIS, NO MEDICAL ADVICE, NO AI
This app does not diagnose, does not suggest causes, does not predict outcomes, and does not recommend treatment. Reports are produced by ordinary arithmetic on the device — there is no language model or external service involved, and no health data leaves the device to generate one. The app describes what the user recorded (counts, averages, date ranges) and explicitly states that these are observations from self-reported entries rather than established relationships.

A medical disclaimer is presented on first launch and must be acknowledged before use. Further disclaimers appear on every generated report.

DATA HANDLING
All data is entered by the user and stored on-device by default. No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers are accessed. There is no analytics or tracking SDK. If the user opts to sign in, data is backed up to Firebase under access rules restricting it to that user's own account.

CONTACT

```
