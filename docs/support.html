Support

Need help with HealthLit? Email support@healthlit.app.

Helpful details to include

  • Your HealthLit app version.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot if it does not contain information you would rather keep private.

Privacy

The current App Store release stores HealthLit data locally on your device and does not require an account. Please do not email private health details unless you intentionally want to share them for support.

Read the HealthLit Privacy Policy.

Medical notice: HealthLit is a symptom diary, not a medical device or emergency service. For medical concerns, contact an appropriate qualified healthcare professional or local emergency service.
